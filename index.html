Duo Cam — Support

Duo Cam - Dual Camera is an iPhone and iPad camera app with photo and video capture, dual-camera composition on supported devices, manual controls, portrait tools, nearby camera control and optional live streaming.

Get help

Open a support issue on GitHub. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps that reproduce the problem. Issues are public: do not include private photos, location details, passwords, stream keys or payment information.

Common questions

  • Camera or microphone unavailable: check Duo Cam permissions in iOS Settings. Microphone access is used when recording sound.
  • Saving or viewing media: allow the appropriate Photos access and check available storage. Photos and videos are managed in your photo library.
  • Dual camera, formats and frame rates: availability depends on device hardware, the chosen cameras and capture mode.
  • Nearby control: use compatible devices, allow local-network access when requested and keep the camera app open. Pair or connect only to devices you trust.
  • Live streaming: enter the server address and stream key supplied by your streaming provider. Start a stream only when you intend to transmit camera content to that destination.
  • Optional tips: open Settings, expand Advanced and choose a tip amount. The five tips are repeatable consumable purchases processed by Apple. They do not unlock camera features or create a subscription. Completed tips are not restorable content; a reinstall may reset local tip-prompt preferences. For billing or refund help, use Apple’s purchase support.

技术支持

请在 GitHub 支持页面提交问题，并附上设备型号、iOS 版本、App 版本及复现步骤。该页面公开可见，请勿发布私人照片、位置、密码、直播密钥或付款信息。

相机、麦克风和相册权限可在 iOS 设置中调整。双摄、分辨率与帧率取决于设备和拍摄模式。附近相机连接需要兼容设备及相应网络权限；直播仅在你主动开始后发送到指定服务器。

五档打赏位于「设置 → 更多/高级设置」，均为可重复购买的消耗型内购，不解锁相机功能，也不是订阅。打赏不提供可恢复的内容；重新安装可能重置本机的打赏提示设置。账单和退款请通过 Apple 处理。
