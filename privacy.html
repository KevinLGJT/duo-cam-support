Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026. This policy applies to Duo Cam - Dual Camera, published by guangjun liao.

Camera content and on-device processing

Duo Cam processes camera images, recorded audio and selected photos on your device to provide photography, video, portrait effects and pose-reference tools. Captured media is saved to your photo library when you choose to save it. The app does not automatically upload your photo library to the developer.

Portrait and pose tools may use Apple Vision to detect faces, facial landmarks, people and segmentation masks on the device. These results are used to render effects and composition guides; they are not used to identify you or build a face-recognition profile. The developer does not receive or retain face or body analysis data. Saved or intentionally shared media may contain the resulting visual effects.

Permissions and location

  • Camera: photography, video capture and live camera preview.
  • Microphone: recording sound for video and enabled streaming features.
  • Photos: saving and viewing media and selecting reference images.
  • Location, while using the app: optionally adding the capture location to saved photos and videos. Location tags may accompany media you later share.
  • Local network and nearby connections: discovering and connecting to camera/controller devices and local streaming receivers.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission can limit the feature that needs it.

Connections you start

When you enable nearby camera control, live preview and control messages are exchanged with the device you connect to. When you start live streaming, media is sent to the server you configure. Streaming configuration and keys are stored using the device Keychain. These destinations may be operated by you or a third party; their privacy and retention practices apply to the content they receive. The developer does not operate a relay or media-storage service for these features.

Optional tips and local settings

Apple processes optional consumable tips through in-app purchase. The app receives product and transaction information and may send the App Store receipt to Apple for verification. Transaction records and settings are stored locally to process purchases and manage tip prompts. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Tips do not create a subscription or unlock camera features.

Tracking, retention and your choices

The app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking, and does not require a developer account. The developer does not operate an analytics service that receives your camera content, location or face analysis. Apple may process App Store purchases and platform diagnostics under its own settings and privacy policy.

Delete saved media through Photos and its Recently Deleted album. Removing Duo Cam removes its app-container data; media in Photos and some system-managed records, including Keychain items and Apple purchase history, may remain. Stop a connection or stream to stop that transmission. Content already received by another device or service is controlled by that recipient.

Support and this website

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, we can see the information you choose to submit. Issues are public, so do not post sensitive information. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub handles website requests and support submissions under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Use the support link for questions about this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.

相机画面、录音、所选照片、人像效果与姿势参考在设备上处理。你选择保存时，照片和视频进入系统相册。App 不会自动将相册上传给开发者。人脸、面部特征点、人体和分割结果仅用于本机效果与构图，不用于身份识别，开发者不会接收或保存这些分析数据。

相机用于拍摄和预览；麦克风用于需要声音的录像或直播；相册用于保存、查看和选取素材；可选位置权限用于将拍摄地点写入照片或视频；本地网络和附近连接用于相机遥控及直播接收设备。你可在 iOS 设置中管理这些权限。分享媒体时，其中的位置标签也可能被分享。

你主动连接附近设备时，预览及控制消息会发送给该设备；主动开始直播时，媒体发送到你设置的服务器。直播配置和密钥通过设备钥匙串保存。接收方的隐私和保存规则适用于其收到的内容，开发者不提供媒体中转或云端存储服务。

App 不含广告或跨 App 跟踪，无需注册开发者账号。开发者不运营接收相机内容、位置或人脸分析的统计服务。Apple 按自身设置和政策处理购买及平台诊断信息。删除 App 会移除其容器数据，但系统相册、钥匙串或 Apple 购买记录可能保留。照片可在系统相册及「最近删除」中删除；停止连接或直播会停止继续传输，已发送内容由接收方管理。

支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。通过 GitHub Issues联系时，你主动提交的信息会对外公开，请勿发布敏感资料。GitHub 按其隐私政策处理网站请求和支持信息。政策变更将在本页更新。
